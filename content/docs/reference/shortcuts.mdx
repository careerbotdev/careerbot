Press G, then the letter, anywhere in CareerBot. <Kbd>?</Kbd> shows every shortcut, and <Kbd>⌘K</Kbd> finds any screen, item or action by name. [Keyboard and ⌘K](/docs/using/keyboard) covers the rest of the keys.
