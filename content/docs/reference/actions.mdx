Every button and menu item in CareerBot carries the same three things in its tooltip, or on a long press on a phone: what it does, what it costs, and whether it can be undone. This page collects them by screen. Actions whose words depend on what's open (a company's name, a count) are left out; their tooltips say the same with the name filled in.
