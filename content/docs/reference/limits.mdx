A limit is something your goals say a job must or mustn't be. **Read my goals** proposes them from your goals story, and you approve, edit or reject each one on Goals, Limits. A firm limit sets a role apart; a preference only ranks it. Anything that can't be checked against a posting goes in the limit's note, which ranks and explains but never filters. [Goals, directions and limits](/docs/how-it-works/goals-directions-limits) explains how they're applied.
