A tailored resume is written for one posting. It starts from your resume for the role's direction and bends it toward what the posting asks for, using only your approved record. Next to it you get a requirements map: how well your record covers each requirement in the posting.

## What you do

1. The step's button, **Open the resume**, opens your pursuit at its **Resume** tab.
2. Choose **Tailor a resume**. The estimate shows first; it uses your AI budget. If you don't have a resume for that direction yet, it's written first in the same run.
3. When it's done, the step shows how many requirements it covers strongly, such as "Tailored · covers 7 of 9 requirements".
4. Read it through and edit any line you couldn't talk to in an interview.

<Shot id="requirements" alt="The requirements of a posting beside a tailored resume: 2 of 4 strong, each requirement marked Strong, Some or None with what it’s built on, and a note where the record falls short." />

If the button isn't there, the role fits no approved direction: "Approve a direction this role fits to tailor a resume to it."

**Tailor it again** writes a new version; earlier versions stay. Once you mark the pursuit Applied, the resume is kept exactly as sent.
