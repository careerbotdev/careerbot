CareerBot writes resumes only from your approved record. Nothing unreviewed, rejected, or taken straight from a story goes in. There are three kinds:

- **Base resume**: a neutral account of your career.
- **A resume for each direction**: the same record, aimed at one kind of work, once the direction and its positioning are approved.
- **Tailored resumes**: one per role you apply to, written from the direction's resume for that posting. See [Tailoring a resume](/docs/start/tailoring).

This step is done once your base resume is written.

## What you do

The step's button, **Open Resumes**, opens your base resume in <Screen id="resumes" />. If it's **Not written yet**, choose **Write**. Writing uses your AI budget; the estimate shows on the button. It runs in the background and appears when it's done.

Read it through. Every line comes from a fact you approved, so if a line is wrong, fix the fact in your record rather than the resume, then write the resume again.

## Once it's written

- **Write again** writes a new version from your record as it is now. Every version is kept; **Compare versions** shows two side by side.
- When facts it rests on change, it says **Changed since it was written**. **Add what’s new** writes just the new lines.
- **Layout** sets how it shows (which roles fold, titles, career breaks), without rewriting anything.
- **Export** gives you **Download PDF**, **Download Word**, **Copy for Google Docs**, **Copy plain text**, and Markdown, HTML or RTF.

Your contact details sit at the top of the base resume, and every resume and letter uses them.

See [Resumes](/docs/using/resumes) and [Resumes, letters and outreach](/docs/how-it-works/writing).
