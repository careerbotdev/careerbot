There are two ways into a role, and they carry equal weight.

- **Outreach**: write to the people who hire. CareerBot finds the hiring manager, people on the team and recruiters at the company, and drafts a short outreach message from your record. You send it from your own email.
- **Apply**: apply through the posting with your tailored resume and a cover letter, then mark the pursuit **Applied**. CareerBot keeps a copy of exactly what you sent.

Most people only apply and wait. A short, thoughtful message to the right person gets you noticed before your application is read, and the team or a recruiter can pass your name on. Doing both is common.

## What you do

Once your resume is tailored, the step **Choose a path** shows two check boxes, **Outreach** and **Apply**. Tick one or both. The same choice is on the pursuit's **Overview**, and you can change it any time.

<Shot id="choose-path" alt="A pursuit’s Overview with the next step Choose a path: two boxes to tick, Outreach with Find contacts and Apply with Open the posting, each saying what it involves." />

Getting started then shows the steps for your path:

- **Outreach**: [Find contacts](/docs/start/people), then [Send your outreach message](/docs/start/outreach-message).
- **Apply**: [Write the cover letter and answers](/docs/start/letters-and-answers), then [Apply and mark it Applied](/docs/start/applying).

The other path stays in the list as **Apply too** or **Outreach too**. Open it to read what it is and add it to the pursuit. Either path finishes your first pursuit; you never have to do both.

A pursuit at a company with no open role (**Start outreach** on a company's details) takes the Outreach path only.

Writing to someone or applying moves the pursuit along on its own: the first outreach message you mark sent sets it to **Contacted**, and **Mark Applied** sets it to **Applied**. A pursuit can be both.
