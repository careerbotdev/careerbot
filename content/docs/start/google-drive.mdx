This step is optional. Connect Google Drive and CareerBot keeps a Google Doc of each resume in a CareerBot folder in your Drive, updated when you keep a new version, ready to share or print. Tailored resumes, cover letters and answers go there too. CareerBot can only see the files it makes.

## What you do

1. On <Screen id="today" />, open **Keep your resumes in Google Drive** and choose **Connect Google Drive**.
2. Google asks you to let CareerBot see the files it makes. Leave that box ticked: without it there's nothing to keep, and you'll see "Google Drive isn’t connected: CareerBot needs access to the files it makes."
3. You come back to Today: "Google Drive is connected. Your resumes are on their way to your CareerBot folder."

Don't want it? Choose **Skip**. You can connect any time in Settings, **Google Drive**.

It's free: syncing uses no AI and no credits.

If the button says "Not available yet", the copy you're using hasn't set up Google yet. On a copy you run yourself, see [Google Drive](/docs/integrations/google-drive).

See [Google Drive](/docs/integrations/google-drive) for what's kept, where, and how to move the folder.
