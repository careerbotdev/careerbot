The first step on the Outreach path. It finds contacts at the company in three groups, all worth writing to:

- **Hiring manager**: the person you'd report to.
- **Team**: people close to the work. They're often quicker to reply, and they can pass your name on.
- **Recruiting**: recruiters and talent people, who can put you in the process.

## What you do

<Steps>
<Step title="Find contacts">
The step's button, **Open People**, opens your pursuit at its **People** tab. Choose **Find contacts**. It's free: Apollo searches for leaders, for people doing the role's own work and for recruiters at the company. They're shown in their groups, worked out from their titles; the most likely hiring manager is marked **Likely hiring manager**. A person's ⋯ menu moves them to another group, and your choice stays when you find again.
</Step>
<Step title="Or add someone you know">
Know someone there already, or Apollo can't look the company up? **Add a contact** takes their name, title, email and group. It's free and needs no Apollo.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Shot id="people" alt="The People tab of a pursuit at Loadstar Systems: the likely hiring manager under Hiring manager, two people under Team each with Reveal email for 1 credit, and the Recruiting group below." />

The step is done once the pursuit has a contact, found or added. The people found also show in the step itself, each with **Reveal email**.
