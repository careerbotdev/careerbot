Your first pursuit starts once Setup is done. Choose one strong fit to go after. Starting a pursuit keeps everything for that role in one place: its resume, the people you write to, your letter and answers, and what happened. Then you tailor your resume, choose a path (Outreach, Apply or both) and follow up.

## What you do

The step shows **Your strongest new roles**, the top three. Choose **Start** beside one. It's free.

Or choose **Open Pursuits** to look at all your roles. Open one and choose **Start** (<Kbd>S</Kbd>). Before deciding you can read how it fits each direction, its full description and what it pays. **Interested** (<Kbd>I</Kbd>) keeps a role on a list for later; **Not for me** (<Kbd>R</Kbd>) sets it aside.

## What a pursuit holds

A pursuit has a status and these tabs: **Overview**, **Role** (the role as it showed before you started it), **Resume**, **Letter**, **Answers**, **People** and **Notes**.

Its status moves from **Preparing** to **Contacted** or **Applied** (a pursuit can be both), **In conversation**, **Interviewing** and **Offer**, or **Closed** with a reason (**Rejected**, **Withdrawn**, **No response**, **Declined**). The Overview suggests the next step for its status and path, for example "Tailor your resume" while it's Preparing, then "Choose a path", and keeps a dated timeline of what happened.

Once you've started one, the rest of Getting started names that company and follows that pursuit.

See [Pursuits](/docs/using/pursuits).
