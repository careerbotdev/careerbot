[Apollo](https://www.apollo.io) is where CareerBot finds companies that fit your directions and the people who work there. It runs on your own Apollo account and spends only the credits you set aside.

## What you do

On <Screen id="today" />, open **Add an Apollo key and a budget**. It asks for three things, saved together:

<Steps>
<Step title="Paste your Apollo key">
Under **Apollo key**, paste a master key, or one with company search, organization enrichment and credit usage. No key yet? **Create one at apollo.io** opens Apollo's API settings.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the credits set aside each month">
**Credits set aside each month** starts at 400. 0 lets CareerBot use whatever is left in your Apollo plan, never more than that.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose how automated Apollo work runs">
**Automated Apollo work** is finding companies, their details and job postings on its own, within those credits: **On**, **Only when I ask**, or **Paused**.
</Step>
<Step title="Save and continue">
**Save and continue** checks the key with Apollo and reads the credits left in your plan. It's free.
</Step>
</Steps>

If the key won't do, you see: "Apollo didn't accept that key, or it can't read your credit usage. Use a master key, or give the key access to credit usage stats, company search and organization enrichment."

## What costs credits

| What | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| A company search (**Find companies**), per page of up to 100 | 1 |
| Finding people at a company | Free |
| Revealing one person's email | 1 |
| Job postings for a company with no public job board | 1 |

Every spend is checked against both your Apollo balance and the credits you set aside. If CareerBot can't read your balance, nothing is spent.

For the full picture (the three modes, what happens when credits run out, the settings) see [Apollo](/docs/integrations/apollo).
