Companies holds every company CareerBot found for your directions, and your rating of each. The open roles of the companies you rate **Target** or **Maybe** are read and ranked into <Screen id="pursuits" />.

## Layout

The list is on the left, the open company beside it. On a phone the list shows alone and a company opens full screen.

The list has five tabs (the last ones fold into **More** when they don't fit):

- **Targets**: companies you rated Target. Their roles are read and ranked first.
- **Maybe**: companies you rated Maybe. Their roles are read too, ranked after your targets'.
- **Found**: new companies, best fit first, waiting for your rating. While their fit is being worked out, the filter bar says how many are ranked so far ("Ranking… 19 of 27") (on a phone, a line above the rows), and the ones not ranked yet wait below the rest in **Not ranked yet**, moving into place as they're ranked.
- **Set aside**: ones you passed on (**Not for me**, with your reason), ones under **Doesn’t fit your goals**, and ones that are **Not a place to work** (staffing agencies, job boards and others that don't hire for themselves). Each says why. Nothing is deleted: **Keep anyway** and **Restore** bring a company back.
- **Archived**: companies you archived, with the day you did. Each shows the list it was archived from; **Restore** (<Kbd>A</Kbd>) puts it back there.

**Show filters** opens the filter bar: by direction, or on Set aside by reason.

**Sort by**, in the list's ⋯ menu, orders Targets, Maybe, Found and Archived: **Best fit** (the default), **Weakest fit first**, **Has a job board first** (best fit within each; their roles can be read), **Newest found** or **Name**. The whole list is sorted, and the choice stays in the address, so a link opens in the same order.

## Archive

**Archive** (<Kbd>A</Kbd>, in a company's ⋯ menu or for the checked rows) clears a company from every list without saying anything about it: your rating stays as it was, nothing is learned from it, and its roles aren't read or shown in Pursuits while it's archived. It's free; <Kbd>U</Kbd> undoes it. That's what sets it apart from **Not for me**, which later searches learn from. Open **Archived** (under **More** when the tabs don't fit) and **Restore** a company to put it back.

## Rate companies

<Steps>
<Step title="Go through Found">Move with <Kbd>J</Kbd> and <Kbd>K</Kbd>. Each company shows **About** (what it does, read from its website), how it fits each of your directions, its **Open roles**, **People** you know there, and **Details**.</Step>
<Step title="Rate it">**Target** (<Kbd>T</Kbd>), **Maybe** (<Kbd>M</Kbd>) or **Not for me** (<Kbd>R</Kbd>). Not for me sets it aside at once. To say why (Industry, Size, Reputation, Place, Not my work, or your own words), choose **Not for me with a note** in the ⋯ menu; later searches use the reason, and **Change the reason** there changes it. Ratings save as you make them; <Kbd>U</Kbd> undoes.</Step>
<Step title="See its roles">**Open its roles in Pursuits** shows them there.</Step>
<Step title="Write to them without an open role">**Start outreach** starts a pursuit at the company with no open role, for the direction it fits best, and opens its People to write to the people who hire there. It's free. It has no tailored resume or cover letter; your direction's resume goes with the message.</Step>
</Steps>

## Details and coverage

Under **Details** are the company's **Website** and **Job board**. <Kbd>E</Kbd> edits them; saving reads the company again from the new address. **Coverage** says how much of the board has been read. **Check again** reads its board afresh and says what it costs first (Apollo credits or AI budget). **Notes** keeps your own notes (<Kbd>N</Kbd>).

**Found** says which direction each search found it for, and how: from its criteria, as one like a company you like, or by the web search, as one that **fits** or is **hiring now**, from a **web search** or **job boards**. **Where it was seen** opens the page the web search saw it on. Until it's filled in, a company the web search found says how in its row ("Fits, web search").

## Find more companies

The list's ⋯ menu (**More for Companies**):

- **Find companies**: searches for companies for each direction with approved criteria. With an Apollo key it searches Apollo, and for ones like the companies you like. Without one, a web search finds companies that fit each direction, hiring or not, and ones hiring now, plus public job boards when you've added a [Brave Search](/docs/integrations/brave) key. It shows each search and what it costs (Apollo credits, or dollars from your AI budget, and the Brave searches) before you start, and under it what the last search found. Your past employers never come back.
- **Fill in details**: reads the website and job board of each company not filled in yet, then how it fits your directions.
- **Sort by**: the order of the list (see above).
- **Select** (or **Select all**): shows a checkbox on each row so you can rate several **Target** together, **Archive** them, or, in the bar's ⋯, rate them **Maybe** or **Not for me** or **Check again**. <Kbd>X</Kbd> on a row, or <Kbd>⇧</Kbd>- or <Kbd>⌘</Kbd>-click, starts selecting with that row checked; <Kbd>⇧</Kbd>-click checks every row between the last one you checked and this one. On a phone, long-press a row to check it and start selecting, then tap to check more. **Done** or <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> stops (see [Selecting several](/docs/using/keyboard#selecting-several)).
- **Search settings** (also **Change** in Find companies, and <Kbd>⌘K</Kbd>): one dialog, full screen on a phone, with Back to Companies.
  - **Companies you like**: start typing a company's name (two letters or more) and matches show as you type, your own companies first, then companies from Wikidata, each with its website. It's free: no Apollo credit, Brave search or AI budget. Pick one to add it, or type its website: the name is read from its own homepage (a public https site only).
  - **Paste a list** (beside the search box, or paste several lines into it): names or websites, one per line or comma separated, up to 100. **Look them up** (free) shows what each line found: a website as it is, a name that matches exactly, or the best match marked **Check this**; lines with **No company found** or **Already in your list**. **Fix** searches a line again, **Remove** drops it, and **Add N companies** adds the rest.
  - Every company you like is added to your companies at every search and used to find more like it (with Apollo, about 1 credit for every 100 looked up, and a lookalike search about 1 credit for every 5; without Apollo, the web search names it as one you like, at no extra cost). A row's ⋯ has **Don't use to find more like it** (the row then says **Not used to find more like it**; **Use to find more like it** turns it back on) and **Remove**, which offers **Undo**. The list's ⋯, beside its title, has **Paste a list** and **Copy list** (their websites, one per line). A direction can add its own in its Criteria, with the same free search.
  - **Industries you want**: **Steer**, **Only these** or **Ignore**.
  - **Check companies against your goals**: **Off**, **Rank** or **Hide**, with **Check all again**.
  - **Learn from your ratings**.

  **Change in Settings** opens Settings, Connections, for the keys, how companies are found and job postings from Apollo.

Company search needs a direction with approved criteria (see [Goals](/docs/using/goals)). An Apollo key is optional (see [Apollo](/docs/integrations/apollo)).

| Key | Does |
