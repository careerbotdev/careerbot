<ScreenFacts screen="settings" />

Settings is a list of sections, each with its current state under its name. The chosen section opens beside the list; on a phone the list comes first and a section opens full screen with a way back. Changes save as you make them, and most can be undone with <Kbd>U</Kbd>. Every section is also in <Kbd>⌘K</Kbd>.

## Account

Your name, how you signed in, and your workspace. **Sign out** signs you out on this device and clears anything you were writing here that isn't saved.

**Contact details** are at the top of your base resume; every resume and letter uses them. **Edit in base resume** opens them there.

On a copy you run yourself (self-hosted), Account also shows your **Username** and **Change password** (your other devices are signed out). The owner also sees **People**: everyone who can sign in to this copy, each with a workspace of their own.

- **Add a person**: you choose their username (2 to 32 letters, digits, dots, dashes or underscores) and get a temporary password to give them. They choose their own password when they first sign in.
- **Reset password**: makes a new temporary password and signs them out everywhere. Can't be undone.
- **Remove**: signs them out and removes their account, then deletes their workspace with everything in it, keys, files and Google Drive and GitHub connections included, in the background ([Deleting your account](/docs/using/your-data#deleting-your-account)). Asks first; can't be undone.

People also says whether Google and GitHub sign-in are on, and that they let in only the emails on the copy's allowlist. See [Sign-in and who can join](/docs/self-hosting/sign-in).

## AI

- **Model**: every task uses this model, from reading your stories to writing letters. Choose from a few recommended ones, each with its estimated monthly cost.
- **Try it**: ask the model one question to check your key and see how it answers, with what it cost.
- **Advanced**: **Tasks with their own model** (**Choose a model for a task**, **Use the main model** to go back), **Sorting roles** (the model that sorts new roles into your directions), and **A model that isn’t listed** (**Choose from all models**).
- **Compare models**: run one task (**Read a story**, **Base resume** or **Insights**) with up to four models side by side, each with its estimate before running, then **Use this model**. Your record and resumes don't change.

See [Models and AI tasks](/docs/ai/models).

## Budgets and spending

- **AI budget**: what's spent this month of your **Monthly budget**. AI work pauses when it's reached and picks up again when you raise it. No AI work runs until you set one.
- On careerbot.dev without your own OpenRouter key: **AI allowance**, the most CareerBot's AI spends for you each month. Your **Monthly budget** can only lower it. When it's used up, a note offers **Ask for more** and **Add your own key** ([more](/docs/ai/budgets#careerbots-ai-on-careerbotdev)).
- **Apollo credits**: credits used this cycle, and what's left in your Apollo plan. **Credits set aside each month**: 0 lets CareerBot use whatever is left in your plan, never more.
- **Automated Apollo work**: **On**, **Only when I ask** or **Paused**. On lets CareerBot find companies, their details and job postings on its own, within the credits above.
- **Open Reports** for spending by month, task and pursuit.

## Connections

Three groups.

### AI

**OpenRouter** key (pays for every AI task; optional on careerbot.dev, where CareerBot's AI is included up to your allowance).

### Companies

- **Finding companies**: Apollo, Apollo + AI or AI only.
- **Apollo** key (optional: finds companies, their details, job postings and people; shows the credits left this cycle; without it, companies are found by a web search).
- **Brave Search** key (optional: finds a company's job board when its website doesn't link one, and searches job boards for companies hiring now).
- **Job postings from Apollo**: when a company has no public job board, ask Apollo for its postings (about 1 Apollo credit per company, while automated Apollo work is on).

Keys show masked. **Add key** or **Replace** checks the key first; the old one keeps working until the new one is checked. **Remove key** asks first and stops that work until you add a key again.

Which companies searches look for (seed companies, companies you'd name, industries, checking against your goals) is in Companies, ⋯ > **Search settings**. See [Companies](/docs/using/companies).

### Resumes and projects

**Google Drive** keeps a Google Doc of each resume in a CareerBot folder in your Drive. CareerBot can only see the files it makes and the folder you choose.

<Step title="Sync">**Sync now** writes what changed and makes again any file deleted in Drive. After a failure it reads **Try again**.</Step>
<Step title="Check the folder">In ⋯, **Open folder** shows it in Drive; **Choose a folder** moves it, with everything in it.</Step>

⋯ > **Disconnect** asks first, stops syncing and ends access; your folder and its Docs stay in Drive.

**GitHub** shows whether it's connected; **Open Projects** to connect it and choose repositories.

Reminders are in Pursuits, ⋯ > **Reminders**. See [Pursuits](/docs/using/pursuits).

## Updates

Only on a copy you run yourself, and only for its owner. Once a day the copy checks careerbot.dev for a newer version
of CareerBot; when one is out, Updates says so (CareerBot 0.8.0 is out, say) with your version, each newer release's
notes, and the steps to update for the way your copy runs (Docker Compose, Coolify, Dokploy or Cloudflare). When any
release between yours and the newest has steps you have to do, it says **Read before updating**: do them as you update.
A line on Today says the same and opens Updates.

**Check for updates** turns the daily check off or on. Nothing about you or your copy is sent, but like any web request
it shows careerbot.dev the IP address of the server your copy runs on; `UPDATE_CHECK=off` where the copy runs turns it
off too. The [changelog](/changelog) has every release; after an update, a note says what version you're on with
**What’s new**.

## Your data

**Export** makes one ZIP of everything in your workspace, to keep or to bring into another copy of CareerBot, and
**Import** brings such a ZIP into an empty workspace. Keys, connections and your password stay behind.
**Delete account** deletes your account and everything in your workspace, once you've typed its name. What's in an
export, how importing works, and what deleting takes: [Your data](/docs/using/your-data).

## Old addresses

Links to sections that moved still work:

- `/settings?section=keys` and `/settings?section=drive` open Settings, Connections.
- `/settings?section=companies` opens Companies with Search settings open.
- `/settings?section=reminders` opens Pursuits.
- `/settings?section=contact` opens Settings, Account.
