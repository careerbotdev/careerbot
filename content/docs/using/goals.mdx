Goals has three pages, under **Goals** in the sidebar: **Goals** (your goals story), **Limits** and **Directions**.

## Goals: your goals story

<Steps>
<Step title="Write your goals">**Write your goals** opens the story. Say what you want next, in your own words: the work, the pay, where, and what to avoid. **Edit the story** (<Kbd>E</Kbd>) changes it; <Kbd>⌘↵</Kbd> saves and <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> cancels. Each save keeps a new version.</Step>
<Step title="Read it">**Read my goals** reads this version for your limits and directions, at the cost shown beside it. What it proposes waits in <Screen id="review" />. Later, **Read my goals again** proposes only what's new; what you approved or rejected stays as it is.</Step>
<Step title="See what it produced">The **Produced** tab lists the limits, directions and company goals that came from each read, what waits in Review, and what was rejected and why.</Step>
</Steps>

**Versions** lists every save, newest first. Open an earlier one to read it; **Restore as newest** brings it back.

## Limits

Rules that filter and rank roles. The list is grouped as **Firm**, **Preference**, **Proposed** and **Rejected**.

- A **Firm** limit hides roles that fail it. A **Preference** keeps them, ranked lower.
- The switch (**Switch off** / **Switch on**) turns a limit off without deleting it.
- A limit shows its sentence, its **Rule** (the values it checks), **What it filters**, and **Roles it hides** or **Roles it ranks lower**, with **See them**.
- If you change the sentence after the rule was set, it says **You changed the sentence after this rule was set**: **Update the rule** reads the rule again (uses your AI budget) or **Keep the rule**.
- **Add a limit** adds one of your own: its kind, your sentence and its rule. It's approved as you save it. **Delete** removes a limit you added (asks first).
- When two limits can't both hold for the same roles, the screen says **Clashes with** the other and why: **Edit** the other one, change this one, or **Keep both**. **Work** and **Companies** limits add up and never clash.

**All your limits** says how many roles they hide and how many they show together.

## Directions

The kinds of work you're going for. Approved directions rank roles; proposed ones wait under **Proposed** with **Approve** and **Reject** on each row. **Reject** takes effect at once and **Undo** takes it back; **Reject with a note**, in the row's menu, asks why first, and your reason steers what's proposed next.

A direction has tabs: **Overview** (what it **Covers**, its fit with your record and its roles), **Criteria** (industries, company sizes, stages, keywords and **Companies you like**: the ones in Search settings, for every direction, shown muted, then **Find more like these for** the direction, with **Remove** on each and the same free search to add one just for this direction, so its searches find companies like it), **Titles** (the **Title map**: the **Target titles** to aim at and the **Market vocabulary** to use) and **Resumes**. Its **Positioning** says how your record is told for it.

- Approving a direction with nothing filled in fills it in on its own: the positioning, titles and criteria, proposed from your approved record. While that runs it says **Filling in**; if it fails, **Fill in** tries again. **Fill in again** proposes new ones for the parts you haven't approved. Positioning and criteria each need approving: **Approve positioning** before the direction's resume can be written, **Approve criteria** before company search runs for it.
- **Show resume** (<Kbd>.</Kbd>) opens the direction's resume beside it. **Tailor a resume** there: paste a posting and get a resume for it.
- **Suggest more** proposes kinds of work your approved record supports that you haven't considered. **Fill in all** fills every approved direction that has nothing filled in, one after another, at about the shown cost each.
- **Merge into…** moves a direction into another you pick, at once and free. Each choice shows what it would gain (new titles, roles and companies) and the pursuits and notes that move with it. The one you pick keeps its name and positioning and gains the other's titles, words to use and criteria, each company and role at its better fit; its roles show **Ranked before the merge** until you **Rank again**, and its resume shows **Record changed**. The merged direction's own resume stays with it. Its companies, roles, pursuits and notes move over in the background, with how far it has got in Activity; until then the kept direction says **Merging…**, and merging, ranking, filling in or rejecting either one waits. **Undo** (<Kbd>U</Kbd>) puts it back as it was, even while that's still under way; while its records move back, both directions say **Undoing the merge…** and the same actions wait.

## Keys

<Kbd>J</Kbd> / <Kbd>K</Kbd> move through each list, <Kbd>E</Kbd> edits, <Kbd>A</Kbd> approves and <Kbd>R</Kbd> rejects a proposed item, <Kbd>⌘↵</Kbd> saves, <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> cancels, <Kbd>.</Kbd> shows or hides a direction's resume.
