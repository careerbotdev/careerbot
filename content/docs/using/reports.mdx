Reports shows what has happened and what it cost. Choose a **Period** (**This month**, **This quarter** or **All time**) and a view. Hover or focus any number, bar or point for what it counts; on a phone, press and hold it.

## Contacted and applied

Outreach and applying are counted apart. **Contacted** counts a pursuit once an outreach message is marked sent or its status is set to Contacted; a pursuit moved past Preparing without applying counts too, since someone there was in touch. **Applied** counts a pursuit once its status is set to Applied. A pursuit can be both, so **Contacted or applied** counts each pursuit once, and the two numbers under it can add up to more.

Interviews are counted against **Contacted or applied**, so an interview that came from outreach counts for its direction. **By resume version** counts applications only, since the resume version is the one sent with an application. On Overview each pursuit counts on the day of its first outreach message or its application, whichever came first; Search and Outcomes count the pursuits started in the period, whatever their status is now.

## Replies

A reply is the first word back from someone at the company: a contact you marked as replied, or a move to **In conversation**, **Interviewing** or **Offer**, or **Closed** as **Rejected** or **Declined**. **Time to first reply** counts the days from your first outreach message or your application, whichever came first.

**Replies by path** compares the pursuits on **Apply**, **Outreach**, and **Apply and Outreach**: how many there are, how many got a reply, the reply rate, interviews, offers and the median days to the first reply. A path shows once it has 4 pursuits, so a handful doesn't read as a pattern. Once both **Apply** and **Outreach** have 4, and one gets at least twice the other's share of replies (2 replies or more), **Suggestions** says so ("Replies come from Outreach", or from Apply).

**Replies by group** shows, for the people you wrote to in each group (**Hiring manager**, **Team**, **Recruiting**), how many replied.

## Layout

On a large screen the list of views is on the left and the chosen view (Overview at first) fills the rest. On a phone the list of views comes first and a view opens full screen.
