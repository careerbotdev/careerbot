CareerBot works on a phone as fully as on a computer. The layout changes below 768 pixels wide; between 768 and 1279 the sidebar is a narrow rail; from 1280 up you get the full sidebar.

## The bottom bar

Instead of the sidebar, a bar sits at the bottom in thumb reach with **Today**, **Review** (with how many decisions wait), **Pursuits** (with how many reminders are due) and **More**.

The bar changes with what you're doing:

- With an item open, it holds that item's actions, the main one filling the rest of the bar.
- While you're selecting rows, it shows how many are checked, what you can do to all of them, and **Done**.
- Before something is deleted, it asks first, with the safe choice focused.
- After a no, it holds the **Why?** field above the keyboard.

It turns back into the areas when you're done.

## More

**More** opens a sheet with the rest: **Companies**, **Resumes**, **Record** with its pages, **Goals** with its pages, **Reports**, the **Activity** row, **Settings** and your account (**Sign out**).

## Lists and items

A screen shows its list alone. Opening a row shows the item full screen, with a way back at the top. What would open beside it on a large screen (a fact's sources, a tailored resume, **Ask about this role**) opens as a sheet.

To act on several rows at once (in Pursuits, Companies, Skills, Tools, Certifications and a Review group's list), press and hold one, or tap **Select** in the list's ⋯ menu. Checkboxes show, a tap checks or unchecks a row, and the bottom bar holds what you can do to the checked ones. **Done** stops selecting.

## Sheets

Menus, pickers, the ⋯ menu, a long-press menu, explanations and ⌘K all open as a sheet that rises from the bottom. Swipe it down, tap outside it, or press <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> to close.

## Press and hold

There's no hover on a phone, so:

- Press and hold a button to read what it does and what it costs, in a sheet titled with its name.
- Press and hold a row for its menu, the same as its ⋯ menu. On a row you can select (see above), pressing and holding checks it and starts selecting instead.
- Press and hold a number or a bar in Reports for what it counts.

## Swiping in Review

A review card swipes: right approves, left rejects. Its other actions sit in the bottom bar.

## Talk

When writing a story or a quick note in Record, or your first story in Getting started, **Talk** types as you speak, using the browser's own speech recognition. Words still being heard show greyed; finished ones go into the text and save when you stop. Nothing is recorded or kept. A phone stops listening when the screen locks or the app goes to the background; what was heard is already in the text. Where the browser can't listen, the screen points to your phone keyboard's own dictation instead.

## Tours

Tours work on a phone too, as a card at the bottom of the screen.
