Today is the first screen you see when you sign in. It answers one question: what needs you now? Each line acts in place, so most days you can work through it without going anywhere else.

## Getting started comes first

Until you put it away, Today shows **Getting started** instead of the list. It has two parts:

- **Setup**: sign in, add your OpenRouter key and AI budget, write your first story, review what CareerBot understood, write your goals, see your base resume, add your Apollo key, find companies, and (optional) connect Google Drive.
- **Your first pursuit**: once Setup is done it folds to one row and the steps of your first pursuit follow, on the pursuit furthest along: pick a role and **Start**, tailor your resume, and **Choose a path**. On the Outreach path, find contacts and send your outreach message; on the Apply path, write the cover letter and answers, then apply and **Mark Applied**. Then **Follow up**. The path you didn't choose stays in the list as **Apply too** or **Outreach too**; either path finishes it.

Each step ticks itself off when the work is done in your workspace. The step you choose opens beside the list with what it unlocks and a **Learn more** link to its page under [First steps](/docs/start). Several steps work in place: you paste your OpenRouter key and set the budget and model right in the step, write your first story there (or **Talk** it), and press **Done, read it** to have it read.

<FirstSteps />

While your workspace is still empty, **Bring your data from another copy** under the steps opens
[Your data](/docs/using/your-data), to import an export from another copy of CareerBot instead of starting over.

To put Getting started away, choose **Hide until later**. Once every step is done, **Show Today** switches to the daily list. **Show getting started** in the screen's ⋯ menu (**More for Today**) brings it back.

## The daily list

The top says the date and how many things need you, or **Nothing needs you**. The lines come in groups:

<Terms>
<Term title="Review">How many decisions wait, with the first groups. Opening it takes you to <Screen id="review" />.</Term>
<Term title="Pursuits">Each pursuit with a reminder due: **Follow up** when an application goes quiet, prepare for an interview, reply to an offer, or **Check in** on one that hasn't moved.</Term>
<Term title="New strong roles">Roles ranked a strong fit for one of your directions in the last week that you haven't rated or started. **Interested** keeps one in mind, **Not for me** sets it aside (with why, if you like), **Start** begins a pursuit.</Term>
<Term title="Resumes">A resume with a new version to keep or discard (**Keep**, **Discard**), or one whose record changed since it was written (**Rewrite**, with its cost).</Term>
<Term title="Working">Work running in the background right now. The same list is in [Activity](/docs/using/activity).</Term>
</Terms>

## Work through it

<Steps>
<Step title="Open a line">Click a line, or move with <Kbd>J</Kbd> and <Kbd>K</Kbd> and press <Kbd>↵</Kbd>. It opens beside the list: the pursuit, the role or the resume update. <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> closes it.</Step>
<Step title="Act on it">A reminder has its done button (for example **Write follow-up** on a quiet application) and **Snooze**, which hides it until the day you pick (**Snooze until**). A strong role has **Start**, **Interested** and **Not for me**. A resume has **Keep**, **Discard** or **Rewrite**.</Step>
<Step title="Go further">**Open in Pursuits** or **Open in Resumes** takes you to the full item.</Step>
</Steps>

Undo is offered in the message after each action, and <Kbd>U</Kbd> undoes the last one.

## Large screens and phones

On a large screen the list is wide on the left and the open line sits beside it. On a phone the list shows alone; a line opens full screen with a way back, and its actions move to the bottom bar. See [On a phone](/docs/using/phone).
