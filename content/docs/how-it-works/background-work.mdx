Most of what CareerBot does takes minutes: reading a story, filling in a few hundred companies, ranking every open role,
writing a tailored resume. It all runs in the background, so you can keep working or close the tab. What it produces
appears as it's ready.

## Activity

**Activity** sits at the bottom of the sidebar (in **More** on a phone). It shows how many things are running, with a
thin progress bar, and opens into a list:

- Running work, with how far along it is where that's known ("24 of 38").
- What failed, with what failed ("Couldn’t read your Brightwater story", "Couldn’t check roles") and the reason under it.
  **Try again** starts it again where that's possible, at about what the first try cost.
- What finished in the last day.

Under the list is this month's spending against your budgets. Waiting work shows why: "Waiting to start",
"Paused until your AI budget allows" or "Paused until your Apollo budget allows".

An AI reply that comes back unreadable, or so long it's cut off, is asked for once more. That second call is paid
like any other and counts in your spending under the same task. If it fails too, the work fails with "The model's reply
wasn't readable. Try again." (or "…was too long and got cut off. Try again.").

## Budgets pause work, they don't fail it

You set a monthly AI budget and the Apollo credits you set aside in <Screen id="settings" />, under
**Budgets and spending**. No AI work runs until an AI budget is set.

Before each paid call, CareerBot reserves what the call could cost. If that would go past what's left, the call doesn't
run and the work pauses. Paused work isn't a failure: it picks up where it left off when you raise the budget, and on
the 1st of each month (UTC), when budgets start again. Nothing that already finished is paid for twice.

Apollo has three modes, under **Automated Apollo work**:

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **On** | Finding companies, their details and job postings runs on its own, within the credits you set aside. |
| **Only when I ask** | Apollo is used only for what you start yourself. |
| **Paused** | No Apollo work runs. |

An Apollo call is never made past your Apollo account's balance, and nothing is spent when the balance can't be read.

## What runs on a schedule

<Terms>
  <Term title="Every morning, 9:00 UTC">The job boards of every workspace with Targets or Maybe companies are read again for new and closed roles, and new roles are sorted and ranked.</Term>
  <Term title="Every ten minutes">With Google Drive connected, your CareerBot folder is compared with what it should hold, and synced only when something differs. Each change also syncs soon after it's made.</Term>
  <Term title="Every five minutes">Work cut off by an update or a crash is picked up again quietly, up to three tries. After that it fails with "This kept getting interrupted. Try again."</Term>
  <Term title="Every hour">Model prices are read again from OpenRouter, so what a call reserves follows the providers' prices.</Term>
  <Term title="Every night">Paid calls whose final cost wasn't known at the time are settled from OpenRouter's and Apollo's own figures, so your spending adds up.</Term>
  <Term title="The 1st of each month">Budgets start again, and work paused for a budget picks up again.</Term>
</Terms>

## What starts on its own, and what waits for you

Some steps follow from what you do; the rest run only when you ask.

On its own:

- After a company search, new companies are screened and then filled in.
- When a story or project has nothing left to review, insights are looked for again.
- When you rate a company or approve, change or switch a limit, the list of roles is brought in step at once (no new AI
  calls).
- The daily roles pass, and Google Drive syncing.

Only when you ask, each with its estimate shown first:

- Reading a story, reading it again, and reading a project.
- **Read my goals**, filling in a direction's positioning and criteria, and **Suggest more**.
- **Find companies**, **Check again** on a company, and **Check all again**.
- **Check roles again** and **Rank again**.
- Writing resumes, letters, answers, messages and follow-ups; **Check against facts** and **Update lines**.
- Gathering skills, finding duplicates and finding the same work.

Only one company search, one fill-in pass and one roles pass run at a time in a workspace. A company you ask to check
again while a roles pass is running is read before that pass ends.
