A company search in <Screen id="companies" /> looks for companies that match your approved directions' search
criteria, through Apollo. Two AI steps then work on what it found: one sorts out the companies that don't hire for
themselves, the other fills in what each remaining company does and how well it fits.

## The steps

<AiStage stage="companies" />

## Screening

<AiStep id="screening" /> runs after each company search. It sorts the companies found into places to work and
everything else: recruiters and staffing agencies, job boards, associations, government, schools, media and investors.
Clear cases are sorted by rule first; the rest by a quick AI read of each company's name and website, and nothing more.

Nothing is deleted. A company it sorts out moves to Set aside, under **Not a place to work**, where you can
**Restore** it. You can also mark a company **Not a place to work** yourself. Screening costs no Apollo credits.

## Filling in

<AiStep id="enrich" /> runs after each search, and when you choose **Fill in details** or **Check again**. It fills in
places to work from free sources first, in batches, strongest-sourced companies first:

- What the company builds. One AI call per batch writes a one-sentence summary from the company's own website text
  only. A site that turns away direct requests is read through a page reader that loads public pages. With no readable
  site, a well-known company gets a summary from the model's general knowledge, labelled as such.
- Its job board. In order: a board link you gave; a link on the company's site or careers page; a web search result
  that names the company, if you added a Brave Search key; a guessed board, kept only if it links back to the company's
  site; and last, only if you turned it on, Apollo's job postings. From the board it counts open roles, remote ones,
  and roles matching your directions' titles inside your approved Location limit.
- Fit. Once details are in, it judges each company against each direction that has approved criteria: strong,
  some, weak or none, with a reason, from the summary, the roles, the direction and your approved limits.

With **Check companies against your goals** set to **Rank** or **Hide** in Settings, the same call also checks each
company against your approved company goals: fits, partly, doesn't, or can't tell, with a reason. It's a signal only.
With **Hide**, ones that don't fit move to **Doesn’t fit your goals**; **Keep anyway** keeps one. Companies you named
yourself are never set aside. **Check all again** in Settings checks every company again against your goals and
directions after you change them.

You decide which companies matter: rate each **Target**, **Maybe** or **Not for me**. Roles are read only from Targets
and Maybe companies. See [Roles](/docs/ai/roles).

## Apollo credits

Apollo bills in credits, on your own Apollo plan. AI steps on this page are paid from your AI budget; Apollo credits are
spent only for these:

- Company searches. **Find companies** shows its estimate in credits before it runs.
- Apollo's job postings for a company with no public job board, about 1 credit per company, only when **Job postings
  from Apollo** is on in Settings.
- Revealing a person's email in a pursuit, 1 credit each, only when you choose to. Finding people is free.

Every Apollo call reads your Apollo balance first and sets aside its credits before it runs. It is refused when your
Apollo plan doesn't have enough left this cycle, or when it would go past **Credits set aside each month** in
**Budgets and spending** (0 means CareerBot may use whatever is left in your plan, never more).

**Automated Apollo work** decides whether Apollo work runs on its own: **On**, **Only when I ask**, or **Paused**. See
[Budgets and costs](/docs/ai/budgets).
