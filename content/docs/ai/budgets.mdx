CareerBot pays for AI with your own OpenRouter key and for company data with your own Apollo plan. Two budgets in
<Screen id="settings" />, **Budgets and spending**, cap what it can spend.

## The monthly AI budget

**Monthly budget** under AI is the most CareerBot may spend on AI in a month. Until you set one, no AI work runs: "Set a
monthly AI budget to continue." The meter shows what's been spent this month and when it resets.

The budget counts calendar months in UTC and starts again on the 1st.

## How a call stays inside it

Before every AI call, CareerBot works out the most that call could cost at its model's current price, and sets that
amount aside against your budget. The call is sent with a cap on its reply, so it can't cost more than it holds. When
it finishes, its real cost replaces what it held, and the rest is given back.

A call is refused, and nothing is sent to OpenRouter, when:

<Messages>
  <Message text="This month's AI budget is used up." where="Anywhere AI runs">Raise the budget, or wait for the 1st.</Message>
  <Message text="This call could cost more than what's left of this month's AI budget." where="Anywhere AI runs">The call's most could go past what's left. Raise the budget to let it run.</Message>
  <Message text="Waiting on AI work already running, which could use the rest of this month's AI budget." where="Anywhere AI runs">Calls running now hold the rest. It starts again by itself as they finish.</Message>
  <Message text="Waiting on earlier AI work to be settled tonight, which could use the rest of this month's AI budget." where="Anywhere AI runs">Calls that were cut off, or didn't say what they cost, hold the rest until the nightly check settles them. It starts again by itself then.</Message>
</Messages>

Prices are read from OpenRouter every hour, so what a call holds follows the providers' prices. A call that may have
cost more than OpenRouter reported is checked each night against OpenRouter's own record, so your spending adds up.

## Pausing and picking up

When the budget runs out, work pauses instead of failing. It picks up where it left off when you raise the budget, and
on the 1st of each month (UTC) when the budget resets. Where an action needs the budget that's spent, its button says
why and offers **Raise budget**, which opens your budgets.

## Estimates beside buttons

Most buttons that start AI work show what it costs beside them, like "About $0.02". The estimate is the average of your
last 20 calls of that kind. Before your first, it says "Uses your AI budget". Hover a button for the same cost.

## Apollo credits

**Credits set aside each month** under Apollo is the most CareerBot may use in each of your Apollo plan's billing cycles.
0 lets it use whatever is left in your plan, never more. Every Apollo call reads your balance first and holds its
credits before it runs. **Automated Apollo work** decides whether Apollo work runs on its own: **On**, **Only when I
ask**, or **Paused**. What spends credits is on [Companies](/docs/ai/companies).

## Where the money went

**Open Reports** in Budgets and spending opens the **Spending** view of <Screen id="reports" />: AI and Apollo spending by
month, by area and by task, who started the work, and spending per pursuit. It counts settled calls; calls still
waiting for their price are listed apart.
