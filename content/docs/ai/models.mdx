Every AI step runs on a model you choose, through your own OpenRouter key. You choose in <Screen id="settings" />, AI.
CareerBot has no built-in model: until you choose one, AI work can't run, and a step says
"Choose a model for reading narratives in settings." (with its own task's name).

## One model for every task

Under **Model**, choose the model every task uses, from reading your stories to writing letters. A few recommended
models are listed, each with a line on what it's good at and what it would cost:

- Before CareerBot has done any work, the cost shows as OpenRouter's price per million tokens read, then written.
- After that, it shows what your last 30 days of AI work would cost a month with that model, at today's price.

To use a model that isn't listed, open **Advanced** and choose it under **A model that isn’t listed**. Any OpenRouter
model works.

## A task's own model

In **Advanced**, **Tasks with their own model** lists the tasks that don't follow the main model. **Choose a model for
a task** gives one its own; **Use the main model** puts it back. Every other task keeps using the main model.

A task with its own model also has its own reasoning effort, for models that take one: the model's default, none, low,
medium or high. Some models reason at length by default, which costs more.

## Sorting roles with Jev

**Sorting roles** in **Advanced** chooses how new roles are sorted into your directions: with the main model (or the
Sorting roles task's own), or with Jev, a decision model made for this. Jev answers, for each role and direction,
whether the role's main work is that direction's work, with a probability. It can't do any other task, so it never
becomes the main model. See [Roles](/docs/ai/roles).

## Try it

**Try it** sends one question to the model, to check your key and see how it answers. It shows the answer, the model,
how long it took and what it cost, and is paid from your AI budget like any call.

## Compare models

**Compare models** runs one task with up to four models side by side: reading a story, writing the base resume, or
finding insights. Each run is paid from your AI budget; nothing it writes enters your record or resumes. When one model
does best, **Use this model** makes it the main model, or that task's own.
