Here is every step you'll meet, in the order you'll meet it: what starts it, what it reads, and what it may and may not
do.

## Three rules

<Terms>
  <Term title="It proposes, you decide">
    What the AI reads from your stories, goals and projects comes back as proposals in <Screen id="review" />. Nothing
    enters your record until you approve it. A rewrite of a fact comes back as a suggestion beside the fact, never as a
    silent change. CareerBot never sends anything for you: you send letters and messages yourself, from your own email.
  </Term>
  <Term title="Writing rests on what you approved">
    Resumes, cover letters, answers and messages are written from your approved record only. Each line cites the
    approved facts it rests on; a citation of anything else is dropped before you see it. A resume line left with no
    approved fact behind it is marked **No approved basis**.
  </Term>
  <Term title="What you rejected stays rejected">
    Rejected items are shown to the AI only so it won't propose them again, never as something true about you.
  </Term>
</Terms>

## What it costs

Every step runs on your own OpenRouter key and is paid from your monthly AI budget. Before each call CareerBot sets
aside the most that call could cost, so it can't spend past your budget. When the budget is used up, work pauses. It
picks up where it left off when you raise the budget, or when the budget resets on the 1st of the month (UTC). Most
buttons that start AI work show what it has cost you lately beside them. See [Budgets and costs](/docs/ai/budgets).

Which model each step uses is your choice, in <Screen id="settings" />. See [Models and AI tasks](/docs/ai/models).

## Outside the order

Two tools in <Screen id="settings" /> never touch your record:

- **Try it** sends one question to the model you chose, to check your key and see how it answers, with what it cost.
- **Compare models** runs one task with up to four models side by side: reading a story, writing the base resume or
  finding insights. You judge the results; your record and resumes don't change.
