## What CareerBot is

CareerBot helps you aim a job search at the right roles and go after each one two ways, with equal weight: apply through the posting with a resume and cover letter written for it, or write straight to the people who hire, the hiring manager, someone on the team or a recruiter. You can do both for the same role. It starts from the record of your career, in your own words: you tell it about each job, it proposes facts, and you approve them. Everything it writes for you, resumes, cover letters, answers and messages, rests only on what you approved, and CareerBot never sends any of it: you do.

It isn't fast and it isn't a resume rewriter. Expect to spend real time on your stories at the start, 20 minutes or more for a job. What you get back is a record you can stand behind, and a search aimed at work that fits it.

CareerBot runs at careerbot.dev, by invitation while it's tested, or as your own copy on your own computer or server. Either way the AI runs on your own OpenRouter key, within a monthly budget you set, and company search runs on your own Apollo key.
